# Instructions for contributors and coding agents

Read README.md and docs/PRODUCT_AND_BUILD_BRIEF.md before implementation. The app is in `clue-agent/` (see README.md for what is built and verified); the other documents are plans. Trading and NEAR Intents funding are planned, not built. Preserve broad prediction and knowledge scope; memecoins are optional topics.

- Build and demonstrate one real end-to-end journey before expanding. Separate planned, simulated and verified features.
- Use current official integration documentation. Retain IDs, retrieval times, resolution terms and market provenance.
- Polymarket is a proposed external venue. Do not build a liquidity pool or imply NEAR Intents supplies its order-book liquidity.
- Never bypass geographic or account restrictions. Never store private keys. Require explicit transaction approval; report partial fills and errors accurately.
- Forecasts and notes are private by default. Do not put private data on a public chain. Sharing and alerts require user choice.
- AI output is not an authority on outcomes or facts. Link original sources, express uncertainty, defend against prompt injection and never claim attestation without verification.
- Do not invent users, prices, volume, performance, partnerships or live integration status. Keep setup and testing claims current.
- Do not add secrets. Inspect third-party agent skills and scripts before use.
- Respect the approved Clue mark and orange, cream, white and ink palette when assets are available. Brand clearance is unverified.
- The meaning of ANS remains unconfirmed. Research before implementing a purported protocol.

## Research agents

Read docs/RESEARCH_AGENTS.md. Agents are not limited to existing Clue pages or markets: support user-directed research on new questions with accessible external sources. The living research page is an optional destination for organizing research. A market is an optional context, not a prerequisite. Preserve citations, uncertainty and privacy, and verify any search provider before claiming it works.
